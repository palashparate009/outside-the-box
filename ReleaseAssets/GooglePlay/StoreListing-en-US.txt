APP NAME
Outside the Box

GAME CATEGORY
Puzzle

SHORT DESCRIPTION (also suitable for the short Play Games description field)
Bend magnetic fields, guide the ball, and solve clever physics puzzles.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Think outside the box. Find the path.

Outside the Box is a physics puzzle game where you guide a steel ball using a magnet around the outside of a glass board. Pull, push, and plan your movements to steer the ball past obstacles and into the goal.

BEAT THE CLOCK
Find an efficient route, manage your energy, and improve your performance to earn more stars. Try a different approach when the obvious path does not work.

EXPLORE THE CAMPAIGN
Play 60 levels across six chapters. Discover new layouts and mechanics, including Echo, magnetic memory, moving obstacles, polarity locks, and orbit goals.

TAKE ON DAILY CHALLENGES
Return for a new daily puzzle and put your magnetic control to the test. Complete the campaign to unlock Endless mode.

MAKE IT YOUR OWN
Customize your ball, magnet, goal, walls, glass, and background. Earn gems through play, discover cosmetic styles, and claim chapter gifts from the store.

KEEP YOUR PROGRESS
Sign in with Google Play Games or an in-game username and password to save your progress in the cloud and compare performance on leaderboards.

A clever solution can start with one small move. Where will you take the ball?

An internet connection is required. Contains advertising and optional in-app purchases for cosmetic items and gem packs. Gems can be used for cosmetics and revives.
